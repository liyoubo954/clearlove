In this context, the safety of subway construction is particularly important. Because the construction work surface is underground, the observation of the construction environment is not intuitive and concealed. The process of shield construction often has great dynamic changes. Due to the complex environment of underground accidents, a variety of risk combinations and connections often lead to large-scale accidents. The causes and results of accidents are often very complicated. The current analysis of the causes of some subway shield accidents does not fully consider the internal evolution mechanism of construction risks, and the occurrence of underground engineering accidents often brings serious economic losses. Therefore, this article makes a systematic analysis of the causes of subway shield construction and accidents, takes the rules and connectivity of the internal risks of the system as the main object of research, and explores the mutual conduction and evolution of various risks in subway shield construction accidents. , And through the risk impact relationship and importance evaluation, trace the key links of the accident, block its internal evolutionary cause chain, and adopt effective methods to reduce the occurrence of subway shield construction accidents, which is of positive research significance. of. Not only that, it also provides new ideas for existing research and supplements risk management programs. This article mainly collects the domestic and foreign subway shield construction accidents in recent decades. With the help of official disclosure or published accident cause investigation reports, the risk point system is established based on the disclosure of accident content and the analysis of the cause of the accident. With the help of the relevant theories of complex networks, the established system is modeled and studied, its internal evolution relationship is explained, the risk evolution mechanism is clarified, and immune measures are proposed. 复杂网络模型往往具有许多单元，类型众多、数量较大，且单元之间的相互关 系较为复杂多变等特征，对如此众多的单元进行研究是一件相对困难的事情，人们 常常基于图论的方法来体现复杂网络理论。方法大致流程是：通过一个图来抽象和 表征一个复杂网络，对其进行抽象化处理，网络中包含系统中的所有节点，每个节 点对应了复杂网络系统中的一个个体，节点之间的连边则代表了网络节点之间的 关系，其定义随着不同网络类型也有所不同。 对这一系统，为了便于计算和储存，网络的拓扑结构通过矩阵来表示，称之为 邻接矩阵，通过研究图的性质，就可以在大尺度的范围内了解整个系统的性质。这 种从研究复杂系统的关系入手来研究复杂系统的方法已经被用于很多社会科学、 工程科学研究领域。 2.2.2 复杂网络模型的特征与类型 通过许多专家学者多年的研究，人们发现复杂网络常常具有一些典型的特征。 In the process of modernization and upgrading of transportation modes in our country, rapid development has been achieved, and it has also effectively alleviated the pressure of urban travel. In this context, the safety of subway construction is particularly important. Because the construction work surface is underground, the observation of the construction environment is not intuitive and concealed. The process of shield construction often has great dynamic changes. Due to the complex environment of underground accidents, a variety of risk combinations and connections often lead to large-scale accidents. The causes and results of accidents are often very complicated. The current analysis of the causes of some subway shield accidents does not fully consider the internal evolution mechanism of construction risks, and the occurrence of underground engineering accidents often brings serious economic losses. Therefore, this article makes a systematic analysis of the causes of subway shield construction and accidents, takes the rules and connectivity of the internal risks of the system as the main object of research, and explores the mutual conduction and evolution of various risks in subway shield construction accidents. , And through the risk impact relationship and importance evaluation, trace the key links of the accident, block its internal evolutionary cause chain, and adopt effective methods to reduce the occurrence of subway shield construction accidents, which is of positive research significance. of. Not only that, it also provides new ideas for existing research and supplements risk management programs. This article mainly collects the domestic and foreign subway shield construction accidents in recent decades. With the help of official disclosure or published accident cause investigation reports, the risk point system is established based on the disclosure of accident content and the analysis of the cause of the accident. With the help of the relevant theories of complex networks, the established system is modeled and studied, its internal evolution relationship is explained, the risk evolution mechanism is clarified, and immune measures are proposed. The main research contents are as follows: 1 Through reference, starting from the collected data of 125 subway shield 北京交通大学硕士专业学位论文 A B S T R A C T vi construction accidents, according to accident data analysis, according to the sequence of the occurrence time of risk points and the causal relationship, the accident chain is extracted from it, and the accident risk point system is established to reveal The evolution mechanism between the related risk points in the accident during the subway shield construction. 2 Use complex network theory to model and simulate the evolution of risks, and use the network model to show the relationship between the risks in the accident and the mutual evolution of the accident. Grasp the overall characteristics of the network through the networks topological characteristic indicators. In this paper, risk points are regarded as the most basic unit in a complex network—nodes; and nodes are connected by unidirectional connected edges, which represent the relationship between risk points. 3 Based on this, a risk evolution model for subway shield construction accidents is established. With the help of a complex network, the relationship of the system can be expressed, and the characteristics of the system function can be studied by analyzing the system structure. In this paper, risk points are regarded as nodes, and the relationship between risk points is edges. The network is composed of 64 nodes and 323 directed weighted edges. By analyzing multiple representative characteristic indicators of the network model, it is verified that the subway shield construction accident risk evolution model has relevant network characteristics, conforms to the characteristics of complex networks, and can reflect the dynamics of risk evolution to a certain extent. 4 Analyze the internal evolution mechanism of risks in accidents, have a deep understanding of the spread and causality of risks in the system, and have a clearer understanding of causality. Due to the need to propose feasible risk control methods, the importance of risk points to the network was simulated, and the importance of risk points was evaluated through different node attack logic, and a more efficient network reduction plan was proposed. The risk avoidance and management put forward constructive suggestions to improve the research system of subway shield construction accidents. 当前，复杂网络已经发展为一门交叉性极强、覆盖多领域的学科，在工程学科 和管理学科中，复杂网络也可以将多学科串联起来。对于复杂网络的定义，学术界 至今还远远没有一个相对完善和严格的物理定义，但在现实世界中，我们能轻而易 举的找到一些非常复杂的系统，例如，一个基因组、一个细胞、一个人类社会等。 在研究较为简单的系统时，人们常常采用还原论的方法。方法大致流程是：对 这一系统抽象化处理，为系统建立一个模型，对模型进行研究，探寻模型的性质， 然后通过实际数据来验证模型与实际情形的对应程度，从而得出实际系统满足这 样性质的可靠程度，进而根据这个模型进行合理的预测。 现在看来，一个有用的办法是：将这些单元抽象成节点，将单元之间的复杂关 系抽象成边，从而整个系统就可以抽象成一个图。通过研究图的性质，就可以在大 复杂网络模型往往具有许多单元，类型众多、数量较大，且单元之间的相互关 系较为复杂多变等特征，对如此众多的单元进行研究是一件相对困难的事情，人们 常常基于图论的方法来体现复杂网络理论。方法大致流程是：通过一个图来抽象和 表征一个复杂网络，对其进行抽象化处理，网络中包含系统中的所有节点，每个节 点对应了复杂网络系统中的一个个体，节点之间的连边则代表了网络节点之间的 关系，其定义随着不同网络类型也有所不同。 对这一系统，为了便于计算和储存，网络的拓扑结构通过矩阵来表示，称之为 邻接矩阵，通过研究图的性质，就可以在大尺度的范围内了解整个系统的性质。这 种从研究复杂系统的关系入手来研究复杂系统的方法已经被用于很多社会科学、 工程科学研究领域。 2.2.2 复杂网络模型的特征与类型 通过许多专家学者多年的研究，人们发现复杂网络常常具有一些典型的特征。 这些特征某些是可以单独存在，在一些领域也是可以多种特征组合存在的。人们依 据复杂网络的特征将网络进行类型划分，如，网络常常具有无标度属性20， 人们 就将具有无标度属性的复杂网络称为无标度网络；网络也常常具有小世界效应19， 人们将具有小世界效应的网络称为小世界网络。但是，存在一些网络既具有小世界 效应，又具有无标度属性，因此按照这样的特征对网络进行分类不是一种严格意义 上的分类。
