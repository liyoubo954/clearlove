implemented as an engineering example, the construction technology of the shield tunneling underneath the Petroleum Pipeline Bridge is introduced. Technical means such as test section and constitutive model calculation software are used to master construction technical parameters of the shield tunnel underneath the petroleum pipeline bridge, scientific monitoring means and necessary secondary grout supplementation and other safeguard measures are adopted, and the construction plan is strictly implemented, which makes the shield tunnel smooth underpass the existing oil pipeline bridge.
