Xiamen 361021，China） Abstract Abstract：When shield tunneling in complex strata，how to master the improvement of the residual soil in the silo in real time and judge the stability of the face is always a problem to be solved. This study mainly starts from solving this problem. Under the premise of auxiliary pneumatic tunneling mode，a video surveillance camera is installed on the cone plate at the top of the diaphragm inside the soil bunker and the corresponding mirror washing system is configured.
