Application of composite weighted cloud model in risk research of outdoor sports events LI Kailing, WEI Liuyang, QU Lu, CAI Jialiang, LI Yao School of Geological Engineering and Geomatics, Changan University, Xian 710000, China Abstract: To reduce the accident rate of outdoor sports events, a combined method of Analytic Hierarchy Process AHP, entropy weight, and cloud model is used to comprehensively evaluate the risk factors of outdoor sports events. Firstly, according to the four elements of comprehensive safety management, a comprehensive evaluation index system composed of three levels is constructed from four aspects: human, machine, environment, and management. The three levels include the module layer, the first-level indicator layer, and the second-level indicator layer in turn. To obtain a more reasonable index system, experts need to be invited and accident cases need to be cited. Secondly, the subjective weight and objective weight of each index can be calculated by the analytic hierarchy process and entropy weight method respectively. Then the two weights are combined and allocated with the appropriate combination method to obtain the comprehensive weight of each index. Thirdly, the standard evaluation cloud and evaluation cloud of each index are calculated based on the cloud model theory. Then, the comprehensive weight is combined and assigned with the evaluation cloud of each index, and the comprehensive evaluation cloud is obtained through calculation. Finally, Matlab software is used to draw the standard cloud map, evaluation cloud map of each index, and comprehensive evaluation cloud map. The evaluation cloud map is compared with the standard cloud map to determine the leading indicators that have a greater impact on event success. Through a comprehensive assessment cloud chart, the overall risk level of outdoor sports events can be determined, achieving the purpose of risk assessment of outdoor sports events. The evaluation method is applied to the Baiyin Marathon accident to verify the reliability and practicability of the method. Besides evaluating the risk factors, this method can also provide a basis for the management and decision-making of outdoor sports events. Application of matter-element extension method in safety risk assessment of subway shield construction FAN Yanyan, WANG Rui School of Civil Engineering, Lanzhou Jiaotong University, Lanzhou 730070, China Abstract: To solve the problems of incomplete results and low accuracy of the safety risk assessment results of the metro shield, a scientific and reasonable assessment of the safety risk of the metro shield is carried out by combining decision-making trial and evaluation laboratory DEMATEL and interpretative structural modeling method ISM with matter-element extension assessment model in this paper. Firstly, based on the literature review and combined with the engineering practice, the safety risk assessment index system of the metro shield is established from personnel and management, mechanical equipment, construction materials, construction technology, and construction environment. Secondly, DEMATEL and ISM are used to analyze the key influencing factors and their degree of influence as well as the interpretation structure relationship among all factors. Thirdly, considering the correlation influence of multiple indicators on the Shapley method is used to determine the index weight to express the comprehensive correlation influence of multiple indicators on the target, and the matter-element extension method is used to build the safety risk assessment model of the metro shield. Finally, the safety risk assessment model of the metro shield of Ningbo Metro Line 3 in the first phase of the Juzhang-Yinzhou passenger transport section is analyzed as an example. The results show that the level of risk assessment is “ moderate risk ” and the risk rank variable characteristic value jis 1. 989, namely the risk in this interval belongs to “moderate risk” and tends to be “low risk”. Through the analysis, the key risk factors are the factors with larger centrality calculated by the DEMATEL method, more node degrees calculated by the ISM method, and higher risk levels calculated by the matter-element extension assessment model, namely the factors of personnel and management, the factors of construction technology and the factors of the construction environment. In the process of shield construction in this area, the managers should pay attention to the geological environment monitoring and strictly do the construction technology work such as foundation reinforcement.
