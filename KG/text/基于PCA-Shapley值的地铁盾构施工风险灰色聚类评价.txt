In order to evaluate the risk of metro shield construction scientifically, a grey clustering evaluation of metro shield construction risk based on PCA Shapley value is constructed. The main component analysis PCA is employed to reduce the dimension of the risk factor set, and the risk evaluation index system is further summarized. Considering the correlation between the indexes, Shapley value method is used to give weight to the risk indexes. At the same time, in order to solve the gray of the risk evaluation, the gray clustering evaluation method is applied to evaluate the construction risk in detail, which reduces the distortion impact on the evaluation results due to the absence of the risk index information. Finally, taking the “An-an” section of Qingdao Metro Line 1 as an example, the risk of shield construction is assessed. The results show that the overall risk assessment level is “low”, in which the construction environment risk level is “general”, and it is necessary to focus on the detection and control of underground geological conditions, aging of underground pipelines and land settlement risks during the construction process of the subway. In order to evaluate the risk of metro shield construction scientifically, a grey clustering evaluation of metro shield construction risk based on PCA Shapley value is constructed. The main component analysis PCA is employed to reduce the dimension of the risk factor set, and the risk evaluation index system is further summarized. Considering the correlation between the indexes, Shapley value method is used to give weight to the risk indexes. At the same time, in order to solve the gray of the risk evaluation, the gray clustering evaluation method is applied to evaluate the construction risk in detail, which reduces the distortion impact on the evaluation results due to the absence of the risk index information. Finally, taking the “An-an” section of Qingdao Metro Line 1 as an example, the risk of shield construction is assessed. The results show that the overall risk assessment level is “ low”, in which the construction environment risk level is “ general”, and it is necessary to focus on the detection and control of underground geological conditions, aging of underground pipelines and land settlement risks during the construction process of the subway.
