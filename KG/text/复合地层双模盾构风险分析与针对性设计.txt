School of Civil Engineering Southwest Jiaotong University Chengdu Sichuan China 3 , , 610031, , Abstract : Adaptive design of dual-mode shield in water-rich gravel-mudstone composited strata is of great significance. As a result, the targeted designs of structure and functional system of slurryearth pressure dual-mode shield used in Wuyi Interchange station-Xinxiu Park station on Nanning Rail Transit Line No. 5 are put forward according to the construction risks such as crossing through Yongjiang River, crossing underneath buildings with shollow foundations, surface settlement induced by shield boring in gravel-mudstone composited strata, and muck cake on cutterhead. As for structural design, the air cushion chamber is used to stabilize slurry pressure, and the slurry discharge port is optimized so as to realize the stable control of surface settlement. The brush and P0 pump circulation systems are set on central panel and bracket to effectively reduce the mud cake phenomenon. The design of screw conveyor is optimized to reduce the gushing pressure, and compared with the adjacent lines, the boring speed of the river-crossing section is increased by nearly one time. As for functional system, mucking system conversion between slurry and earth pressure dual-mode is realized, the total thrust of the propulsion system reaches 39 910 kN, which can meet relevant requirements under different working conditions, the surface settlement is strictly controlled according to grouting system and rational tunneling parameters control. School of Civil Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, Sichuan, China Abstract: Adaptive design of dual-mode shield in water-rich gravel-mudstone composited strata is of great significance. As a result, the targeted designs of structure and functional system of slurry earth pressure dual-mode shield used in Wuyi Interchange station-Xinxiu Park station on Nanning Rail Transit Line No. 5 are put forward according to the construction risks such as crossing through Yongjiang River, crossing underneath buildings with shollow foundations, surface settlement induced by shield boring in gravel-mudstone composited strata, and muck cake on cutterhead. As for structural design, the air cushion chamber is used to stabilize slurry pressure, and the slurry discharge port is optimized so as to realize the stable control of surface settlement. The brush and P0 pump circulation systems are set on central panel and bracket to effectively reduce the mud cake phenomenon. The design of screw conveyor is optimized to reduce the gushing pressure, and compared with the adjacent lines, the boring speed of the river-crossing section is increased by nearly one time. As for functional system, mucking system conversion between slurry and earth pressure dual-mode is realized, the total thrust of the propulsion system reaches 39 910 kN, which can meet relevant requirements under different working conditions, the surface settlement is strictly controlled according to grouting system and rational tunneling parameters control. 在稳定地层,原地缓慢旋转刀盘,用螺旋输送 机尽量把土仓内渣土清除,通过向土仓内注入膨润土 弥补排渣带来的土仓压力降低。 当土仓上、下部压力基本一致时,关闭螺旋输 送机和皮带机,螺旋输送机轴收回,关闭土仓前闸门和 螺机后闸门。 打开主进浆球阀,环流系统开启旁通模式往土 仓内加浆,同时打开排气管进行排气,直至排气管喷 浆,浆液灌满整个土仓。 环流系统土仓循环,直至土仓循环顺畅无问 题,转为泥水模式掘进,先慢速掘进,根据出渣情况逐 步提高推进速度至正常掘进状态。 2泥水模式转土压模式掘进 在稳定地层,关闭气垫仓与泥水仓间的联通管 路,对泥水仓进行保压测试。 用低比重泥浆在土仓及管路之间循环,尽可能 排除渣土。 调试螺旋输送机和皮带机至工作正常,打开土 仓前闸门,伸长螺杆至土仓内部。 运行环流旁通模式并缓慢推进,利用进浆管排 浆,让渣土在土仓内缓慢堆积,当泥水分离设备有土渣 溢出时,堆渣完成。 开启螺旋输送机后闸门,根据喷浆情况判断出 渣状态,当泥浆明显减少,塑流状渣土开始间歇性排出 时,则出渣状态达到平稳。 出渣状态平稳后,开启泡沫系统、膨润土系统 进行渣土改良。待土仓渣土降到合适位置,提高推进 速度,完成模式切换。 4. 2 推进系统针对性设计 双模盾构配备推进油缸30 根,每根推力1 500 kN 35 MPa,油缸行程2 150 mm,合计推力39 910
