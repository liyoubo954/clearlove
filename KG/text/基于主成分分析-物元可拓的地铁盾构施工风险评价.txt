University Research Center for Smart City Construction and Management of Shandong, Qingdao 266520, China Abstract: Aiming at the problems of insufficient reliability of metro shield construction risk evaluation index system and low accuracy of evaluation results, an improved evaluation model based on matter- element extension method is proposed to perform scientific and effective metro shield construction risk evaluation. Firstly, the dimension of risk factor set is reduced by the method of main component analysis, and a metro shield construction risk evaluation index system is constructed from four aspects of personnel management, construction technology, mechanical materials and environment. Secondly, considering the criterion weight and order weight, the weight of risk index is determined in combination with OWA operator and simple correlation function method. Then considering the fuzziness of the quantification of risk factor, triangular fuzzy number is introduced to construct the construction risk evaluation model based on matter-element extension evaluation method. Finally, the model is applied to evaluate the shield construction risk of the An-An Section of Qingdao Metro Line 1. The results show that the general risk assessment level is “ low risk ”. Among them, technical risk and environmental risk are relatively high. During the shield construction period of this project, geological environments, such as water level fluctuation, should be monitored, and construction techniques such as sealing and waterproofing should be strictly performed. 因素进行降维处理,并结合项目实际情况,建立了地铁 盾构施工风险评价指标体系,确保了指标体系的科学 合理性。 2针对指标权重,综合考虑了准则权重和次序 权重的优劣,采用OWA 算子法和简单关联函数法相 结合的综合权重法确定了指标权重,以增加结果的准 确性。 3针对盾构施工风险因素的模糊性、不确定性 及专家评价的偏好差异性,引入三角模糊数和专家动 态权重进行处理,并建立了相应的五级语义转化集,以 增加评价信息的完整性和准确性。 4借助物元可拓理论解决风险因素评级的矛盾 性,构建物元可拓综合评价模型对地铁盾构施工风险 进行评价,以增强结果的准确性。该模型可为地铁盾 构施工提供较为精确的评价信息,同时也可以拓展到 其他领域,具有一定的借鉴意义。 地铁盾构施工风险因素众多且复杂,未来可深入 研究具体风险指标值的变化对于目标的影响程度。此 外,本次主要研究了地铁盾构施工风险评价问题,对盾 构施工风险的应对措施研究不足,后续可对此深入研 究,以推动我国地铁项目盾构法施工的发展。 参考文献: 1 徐顺明. 广州轨道交通盾构隧道施工控制测量的研究D. 武汉: 武汉大学,2012. 2 EINSTEIN H H.
