Safety Risk Assessment of Shielddriven Tunnel Undercrossing Railway Based on Cloud Model and Improved Evidence Theory YAO Chunqiao1，2，WANG Jinfeng1，YANG Sai2，WU Xianguo2，CHEN Hongyu3 1.Wuhan Metro Group Co. Ltd.，Wuhan 430070，China； 2.School of Civil and Hydraulic Engineering，Huazhong University of Science and Technology，Wuhan 430074，China； 3.School of Civil Engineering and Environment，Nanyang Technological University，Singapore 639798，Singapore Abstract In order to evaluate the safety risk level of shield-driven tunnel undercrossing railway complex system，a hybrid method based on cloud model，improved evidence theory and Monte Carlo simulation was proposed. A set of safety risk evaluation index system and evaluation criteria covering 14 influencing factors were established. Through the calculation of correlation measurement of cloud model，the basic probability assignment of different influencing factors in different risk states was constructed to obtain the evidence set. According to the consideration of evidence quality，the evidence was screened and preprocessed，and the combination of multi-source and conflict evidence was realized based on the improved evidence combination rules，so as to obtain more accurate and reliable risk perception results，and provide risk management support and decision-making basis for the safe construction of shield-driven tunnel. Monte Carlo simulation method was used to analyze the global sensitivity of the influencing factors and determine the key influencing factors that have a greater impact on the final risk state. Finally，the applicability and effectiveness of the safety risk analysis method were verified by taking a metro shield-driven tunnel undercrossing the railway track group as an example.
