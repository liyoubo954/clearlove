Abstract: This paper evaluates and ranks the risks of shield construction by using the analytic hierarchy process AHP and comprehensive index method to examine the characteristics of Zhengzhou metro line 6 and the soil layer undercrossing exist railways in several sections. Then the high-risk sections are selected to establish the numerical shield excavation model and analyze the soil layers deformation characteristics. The combined use of these methods can provide reference and new ideas for the construction and risk management-control of similar projects.
