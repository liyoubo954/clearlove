Construction Risk and Preventive Measures for Subway Shield in Guiyang’s Karst Area MO Weiping （Guiyang Urban Rail Transit Group Co. Ltd.，Guiyang 550081，China） Abstract Taking the shield construction between Zaojiaojing station-Taiciqiao station of Guiyang rail transit line 3 as an example，seven kinds of risks existing in shield construction in moderately weathered dolomite and limestone stratum in Karst area of Guiyang were introduced in detail，and corresponding preventive measures for possible quality and safety problems in construction were put forward. Based on the analysis of the tunneling parameters of the abnormal conditions such as tool wear，edge collapse，bolt loosening and cutterhead paste in the similar stratum of the constructed Sifanghelu station-Zaojiaojing station shield interval，the tunneling parameters suitable for the stratum were given. It is pointed out that the information construction can realize the real-time monitoring and dynamic management of the shield tunneling process，and optimize the construction quality，safety and economic benefits.
