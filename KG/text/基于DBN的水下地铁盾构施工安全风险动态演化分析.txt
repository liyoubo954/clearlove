Abstract: In order to establish a systematic and complete safety risk index system for underwater metro shield construction，and carry out in-depth research on the dynamic evolution of risk，WBS-RBS method is used to establish a risk index system for the whole process in the perspective of human，machine， environment and management A dynamic evolution model of safety risk for underwater subway shield construction is constructed based on Dynamic Bayesian Network DBN Combined with the probabilistic change process of risk with time，the risks in different situations are predicted by forward and reverse reasoning Dynamic evolution path Applying this model to the case analysis of the Chengdu Metro，the results show that the probability of environmental risk is high when it is constructed under high water pressure due to the fact that the underwater metro shield needs to cross the river The occurrence of environmental risk will not only affect other risks at the current time node，but also cause human， mechanical and other risks in the subsequent construction，which may finally lead to the occurrence of risk events The consistence of analysis results with the actual situations proves the correctness of the model The quantitative analysis of the dynamic evolution process of risk provides a more practical method for the safety management of underwater tunnel shield construction Key
