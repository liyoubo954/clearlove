Southwest Survey and Design Co., Ltd., of China Railway Fourth Institute Group, Kunming, Yunnan 650206, China Abstract: As urban rail transit construction increases year by year, conflicts between new tunnels and existed bridges become increasingly obvious. Aiming at the risk assessment problem of shield tunnel crossing the existing bridge construction, and based on the system idea, we introduce the C-V-T model to classify the engineering risk into consequence severity, which is called C; the vulnerability of the existing bridge system, which is called V; and the threat of the new tunnel system, which is referred to as T, so as to construct a three-system risk assessment process of disaster-causing, disaster-bearing and post-disaster. By introducing the two-factor catastrophe theory to establish a three-level index system, we obtain the catastrophe progression of the existing bridge to express its vulnerability. In order to improve the accuracy of T-value, we use the interaction matrix to measure the cross influence between the indicators of the new tunnel, and then give the indicator-system weight method. Finally, introducing the complex mapping relationship and transformation situation between the quantitative indicators and qualitative concepts to express multi-dimensional cloud and forward generator optimization algorithm objectively. Additionally, we clarify the consequence severity grading criteria, by which defining the direct economic loss, the surrounding environmental loss and human casualty, give the coupling method of the three system risk assessment results, and thus build a comprehensive assessment model for the construction risk of shield crossing existing bridges. We apply the proposed model to the risk assessment of Yixin Bridge Crossing Project of Kunming Metro Line 5, which shows that the evaluation results match with the actual situation of the project. The construction monitoring data also verifies the rationality, standardization and applicability of this model. Southwest Survey and Design Co., Ltd., of China Railway Fourth Institute Group, Kunming, Yunnan 650206, China Abstract: As urban rail transit construction increases year by year, conflicts between new tunnels and existed bridges become increasingly obvious. Aiming at the risk assessment problem of shield tunnel crossing the existing bridge construction, and based on the system idea, we introduce the C-V-T model to classify the engineering risk into consequence severity, which is called C; the vulnerability of the existing bridge system, which is called V; and the threat of the new tunnel system, which is referred to as T, so as to construct a three-system risk assessment process of disaster-causing, disaster-bearing and post-disaster. By introducing the two-factor catastrophe theory to establish a three-level index system, we obtain the catastrophe progression of the existing bridge to express its vulnerability. In order to improve the accuracy of T-value, we use the interaction matrix to measure the cross influence between the indicators of the new tunnel, and then give the indicator-system weight method. Finally, introducing the complex mapping relationship and transformation situation between the quantitative indicators and qualitative concepts to express multi-dimensional cloud and forward generator optimization algorithm objectively. Additionally, we clarify the consequence severity grading criteria, by which defining the direct economic loss, the surrounding environmental loss and human casualty, give the coupling method of the three system risk assessment results, and thus build a comprehensive assessment model for the construction risk of shield crossing existing bridges. We apply the proposed model to the risk assessment of Yixin Bridge Crossing Project of Kunming Metro Line 5, which shows that the evaluation results match with the actual situation of the project. The construction monitoring data also verifies the rationality, standardization and applicability of this model. Liu5在对风险评估模型的研究中指出风险评估应 从致灾、承灾与灾后3 个角度进行分析，并给出了 风险定量计算公式，为建立全面可靠的评估流程提 供了参考。卢春林6对Kent 法进行改进并用于浅埋 暗挖隧道穿越桥梁风险评估中，提出了以风险综合 指数CRCI 定量表示施工风险程度，但其存在指标 划分混乱，取值模糊，主观性过高等问题。阮永芬 等7引入云模型算法并优化了权重计算法，构建了 组合赋权云评估体系。但一维云处理多参数交互问 题的效率较低，从而导致评估结果与实际不符。卢 鑫月等8将动态贝叶斯网络与模糊评价理论相结合， 建立了地铁隧道施工动态风险评估，但仅对施工状 态进行评估，所得结果是否全面严谨还有待验证。 其次，各项研究对风险评估的定义及流程存在较 大出入。国家标准《系统安全性通用大纲》 （GJB 900 90）9及《城市轨道交通地下工程建设风险管理规 范》（GB 506522011）10中定义风险R T C ， 即风险R 为不利事件或事故发生的概率（频率）T 及其损失C 的组合。或是从系统脆弱性出发6，将 风险定义为承灾系统脆弱性与后果严重性综合作用 的结果。Liu5、Arnold 等11在总结分析了传统风险 评估模型后均认为，风险评估应重点考虑三要素， 即致灾系统、承灾系统与灾害后果。归纳现有研究 成果可知，随着信息与计算机技术的迅速发展，常 规评估方法或规范公式存在明显局限性，不能真实 反映待评估系统特性，无法充分有效地利用施工、 监测及勘察数据。同时，现有评估模型存在一定的 评估指标考虑不全面，指标划分不明确，主观性较 大，无法解决多参交互作用等问题，从而导致模型 适用性低，工程应用受限。 为此，本文针对盾构穿越工程施工特性引入 C-V-T 评估模型，将风险评估划分为后果严重性 （C）、既有桥梁系统脆弱性（V）及新建隧道系统 威胁性（T）三方面。针对既有桥梁承灾特性引入 双因素突变理论构建多级指标体系，以真实反映桥 梁系统突变风险。引入交互矩阵评判新建隧道系统 多因素交叉、综合影响系统威胁性特征，并给出指 标权算法。此外，将一维云拓展至多维真实反映多 定量指标与多定性概念间复杂映射关系，建立交互 作用影响下7 维云综合评估模型。将三系统评估结 果耦合得出最终风险评估等级，由此建立盾构穿越 既有桥梁施工风险评估模型及控制措施实施流程， 辅助工程施工方案调整。将该风险评估模型应用于 昆明地铁5 号线实际穿越工程工前评估中，对比分 析结果与工后实际情况，从而验证模型的严谨性、 合理性及适用性。 2 C-V-T 风险评估模型 2.1 评估模型的构建 风险评估近年来发展迅速，其是对一系列社会 活动带来的风险程度、后果严重性等进行评估并控 制以实现防灾减灾目的，被广泛应用于岩土、采矿、 服了传统一维云计算繁琐、主观影响大与指标不全 等缺点。最终耦合三系统评估等级得出综合风险评 估结果，由此建立的评估模型定义明确、指标全面、 算法合理，所得结果可精准表达工程施工风险状况。 （4）将该模型应用于实际穿越工程中，分析结 论与工程实际情况吻合，有效保证了工程计划的顺 利实施。评估结果显示新建隧道覆跨比、施工管理 及辅助加固措施对穿越工程风险性存在较大影响， 积极实施隔离桩与动态注浆可有效控制既有桥梁在 施工过程中的变形。该模型可对多种方案进行评估， 优化其不利指标并与监测数据结合动态更新风险级 别，为施工参数调整提供有力支持。该评估模型及 控制体系为风险评估提供了新的角度，可为近似工 程提供借鉴与参考。 另外，本文模型指标体系与计算逻辑是基于案 例工程构建的。当场地环境与工程情况变化后，可 能存在需增、减部分指标，适用性会有所降低的情 况。故应用本文模型时，可针对具体工程特性适当 调整指标及计算逻辑。同时，本文模型仅考虑了施 工阶段的风险性，应进一步从轨道交通设计、施工 及运行多方位全过程出发，对待建轨道交通系统开 展风险评估研究并实施控制，以期加强各相关学科 间联系，实现时空效应下设计规范施工、施工考虑 运营需求、运营需求指导工程设计的全闭环实施。 参 考 文 献 1 毛保华, 姜帆, 刘迁, 等. 城市轨道交通M. 北京:
